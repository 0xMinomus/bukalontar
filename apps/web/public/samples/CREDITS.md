## Changes to the selectable examples

Coordinates refer to original pixels, `(left, top, right, bottom)`, with right/bottom exclusive. Each crop preserves the entire front-side leaf, removes the other side and surrounding table, then resizes to 2400 px wide with Lanczos resampling and JPEG quality 95 (no chroma subsampling). No glyphs are generated, retouched, or reconstructed.

The Wanagiri and Sukawati photographs remain for existing decorative/sharing assets and historical evaluations, not as selectable examples. A rejection by Gemini is not proof that a photograph uses Kawi script. Source transcription validation does not validate the model's generated reading.
